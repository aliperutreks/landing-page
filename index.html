Trust & safety

A fully authorized tour operator in Peru

We operate under the strict legal and safety standards required by the Peruvian government. We hold registration with DIRCETUR (Regional Directorate of Foreign Trade and Tourism of Cusco), recognition from MINCETUR, SERNANP authorization to operate within the Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu and the Inca Trail network, and a valid municipal license. This means legitimate permits, guides trained on up-to-date safety protocols, and porters hired under fair working conditions.
